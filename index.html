/FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers about working together, timelines, and what to expect.

I focus on four core areas: Web Designing (clean, conversion-focused interfaces), Web Development (fast, accessible front-end builds), SEO (technical foundations + on-page strategy), and CMS for Business (WordPress, Webflow, or custom setups that clients can actually manage).

Most projects blend two or more of these. If you need something outside this scope I’ll tell you early and point you in the right direction.

A focused landing page or small marketing site usually lands in 2–4 weeks. Full multi-page websites with custom interactions sit around 5–8 weeks. Larger CMS builds or projects that need content strategy can stretch to 10–12 weeks.

Timeline depends on how quickly feedback and content arrive from your side. I always share a clear schedule at the start so there are no surprises.

Yes. Almost all of my work is remote. I’m comfortable with clients across different time zones and use async communication (Loom, Notion, email) so nothing waits on a live meeting.

We can schedule calls when it makes sense, but the process is designed so progress continues even when we’re not online at the same time.

Every project includes strategy kickoff, design system or key screens, development, basic SEO setup, performance checks, and a handoff with documentation so you (or your team) can update content later.

Source files, component notes, and a short training session are part of the package. Ongoing maintenance or new feature work can be arranged separately after launch.

Send a short note about what you’re building and where you are in the process. I’ll reply within a couple of days with a few clarifying questions and, if it looks like a fit, a proposed scope and timeline.

No long forms or sales calls required up front — just a clear conversation so we both know it’s the right match.
