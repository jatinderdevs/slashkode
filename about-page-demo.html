The story

From freelancer to Melbourne agency

For several years I worked as a freelance web developer and designer under JatinderDev, helping Melbourne businesses — especially in the VET and education space — get clear, reliable websites and systems that worked for them.

In 2024 I registered Slashkode as a sole trader with ASIC. The goal was simple: keep the same hands-on approach clients liked, but build a clearer brand that can grow with the businesses I support.

Today Slashkode focuses on websites, custom web applications and practical digital support for Melbourne businesses that want something honest, useful and built to last — not just another pretty template.
